Overview

The system’s only Earth-like, living world — and a death trap. Its flora and fauna home in on electromagnetic emissions; you work EM-silent or you die. The same hazard that keeps everyone away makes it the perfect place to hide something.

Points of Interest

EM-silent landing zones, and the living wilds. The same hazard that keeps everyone away makes it the perfect place to hide something.

Appearances

  • Session 1 (Session 01 - The Ragged Edge of Space) — Named only in Chief Holger Graeme’s briefing: a federally restricted world with deadly flora and fauna, its orbital satellites needing occasional maintenance escorts. Not visited.
  • Session 3 ([Session 03 - Buckle Up Motherfuckers](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 03/session-03-buckle-up-motherfuckers.html)) — Visited. The crew ran the relay-servicing circuit across its biospheres (mountain, desert, highland, jungle) before their transport was downed by a dissolving substance. On the surface they fought the world’s EM-drawn wildlife (Furry Lampreys, a Chitinoteuthis), discovered the sabotaged Relay Station 11, and raided the hidden Kinetic Logistics Compound before escaping orbit with a stolen ship and a prisoner.

Session 3 — Buckle Up, Motherfuckers

The crew’s first time on the surface. Player-observed detail: relay platforms are forty-to-fifty-foot concrete towers ringed by EM diffusers and accessed by retractable ladders; the wildlife (Furry Lampreys, Chitinoteuthis) homes on electromagnetic signatures and is held off only by the diffusers. The relay network is compromised — Relay Station 11 was falsely logged as serviced for two years — and a hidden Kinetic Logistics Compound operates in the world’s EM shadow, with ships jumping directly into the atmosphere on vortex drives that bypass the sentinel satellites. See [Session 03 - Buckle Up Motherfuckers](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 03/session-03-buckle-up-motherfuckers.html).

Session 2 — The MacMillian Anomaly

The crew’s next assignment: a maintenance-escort run here, servicing relay arrays on 20-ft concrete platforms and geosynchronous satellites. Player-side, the crew read the posting as a political maneuver to get them off MacMillian Station IV while Peter Goodwin was still visiting. See [Session 02 - The MacMillian Anomaly](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 02/session-02-the-macmillian-anomaly.html).

mobRPG (canon import)

A habitable terrestrial world that possesses a breathable atmosphere, Thides III is unoccupied by being declared a restricted planet, due to dangerous flora and fauna. Many of the creatures, and some of the plant, have a natural affinity for electromagnetic waves. These seem to draw the flora and fauna toward sources of the waves. This draw, along with the deadly nature of these creatures, makes establishment of any human activity and difficult, leading Federal scientists to place restrictions on the plant. It is considered a level 1 deadly restrictions due to the flora and fauna.

Relationships

  • Part of Thides System — Third world; the system's only living biosphere.

Places Within

Kinetic Logistics Compound

A large, illegally established base on the surface of restricted Thides III, roughly five miles from Relay Station 11 and glowing with the same yellow diffuser light as the relay platforms — the EM-shield that lets people work on a world whose wildlife hunts by electromagnetic signature.

Relay Station 11

A remote communications array atop a fifty-foot concrete platform in the jungle of Thides III — one of the relay stations the crew's maintenance run was meant to service.
