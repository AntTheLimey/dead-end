Docking Bay 36

Docking bay / hangarA working hangar of autonomous loader robots and crated cargo
A large hangar aboard MacMillian Station IV where the crew's stripped-down Type 113A transport was docked and loaded — by autonomous robots — with maintenance equipment for the Thides III run.

Overview

A large hangar aboard MacMillian Station IV where the crew’s stripped-down Type 113A transport was docked and loaded — by autonomous robots — with maintenance equipment for the Thides III run. The crew met Jareth Brand here and departed from it; Ronin Sanchez nearly started an incident trying to fire the engines inside the bay before Jareth radioed for clearance.

Points of Interest

Appearances

  • Session 3 ([Session 03 - Buckle Up Motherfuckers](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 03/session-03-buckle-up-motherfuckers.html)) — Departure point for the Thides III mission.

  • Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — The Stolen Alien-Tech Transport docked here under Julija Borja’s quarantine order, guided in by station ROVs. Six declined to wait for the docking ring and crossed the vacuum to the station airlock on his hand thrusters before the umbilical had finished extending. Holger Graeme met the crew at the dock with two officers and locked the ship down. By the end of the session the ship had left the Thides System entirely — out of a federal quarantine, from this bay.
