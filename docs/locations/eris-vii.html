Overview

Positioned at the very edge of the Orange Dwarf’s light, this world is a freezing wasteland of nitrogen ice, methane snow, and jagged obsidian-like rock formations. Its highly eccentric orbit occasionally pulls it inside the path of Eris VI, causing temporary atmospheric thaws.
