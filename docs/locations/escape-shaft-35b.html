Escape Shaft 35B

restricted catwalkDark, dusty, and abandoned — a mothballed catwalk overlooking the Main Line, stripped of anything a scavenger could carry off
A restricted, sealed catwalk system running above The Main Line in Processing I, reached through an emergency exit door in Section 102.

Overview

A restricted, sealed catwalk system running above The Main Line in Processing I, reached through an emergency exit door in Section 102. The area is mothballed and shows clear signs of scavenger activity — stripped copper wiring, salvaged electronics, and dust-covered notepads left behind by whoever once worked the catwalk.

On [Session 2](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 02/session-02-the-macmillian-anomaly.html), a Nova Nexus security guard sent Six, Rock Lavey, and Ronin Sanchez here with a fake lead on the illegal fighting pits, using the orange-handled door in Section 102. Opening the sealed door tripped a station-wide alarm; Six cancelled it from his security terminal, though a secondary team was still dispatched to check. Inside, the crew found a dark corridor leading to a stairwell, and Six and Rock Lavey climbed up onto the catwalk itself, overlooking the Main Line below. Shackleton Magellan caught up with the group as they realized the catwalk led on toward the shipping and receiving levels, and the team pulled back out of the restricted area rather than risk further security complications.

Rock Lavey and Shackleton Magellan, comparing notes afterward, concluded the guard had sent them here purely to get rid of them — there had never been a fighting pit lead to find.
