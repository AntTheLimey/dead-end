Corrosive Goo

consumable

Current Holder: none — dissipated/retreated with the container

Origin: Released from ruptured cylinders in the back of the Kinetic Logistics Container

Overview

Cylinders mounted at the back of the Kinetic Logistics Container ruptured moments after Six began cutting into the relay wiring, releasing a dark ooze that spread fast and aggressively consumed the electronics around it — chewing through wiring, terminals, and the alien-side hardware alike. It behaved less like a leak and more like a system responding to intrusion: everything it touched came apart.

The substance briefly touched Six’s vibro-axe during the fighting retreat to clear the container, leaving a faint cosmetic scar on the blade’s edge before the ooze retreated. It never reached Six directly, and it vanished along with the container and the rest of the relay when the countdown expired.

Nobody on the crew got a sample. Whatever it was built to do, it did its job — by the time station sensors recorded the vortex event, there was nothing left of the relay for the ooze to still be eating.

Properties

  • Appearance: dark, viscous ooze.
  • Trigger: released from ruptured cylinders at the rear of the container, apparently in response to the relay being tampered with or the countdown reaching its final stage.
  • Effect: aggressively disintegrated nearby electronics; left only a cosmetic mark where it briefly touched Six’s vibro-axe before retreating.
  • Sample status: none recovered — no trace survived the container’s disappearance.
