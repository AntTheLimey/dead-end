Alien Navigation Device

relic

Current Holder: Gone with the ship — location unknown

Origin: Found integrated into the navigation computer of the Stolen Alien-Tech Transport, taken from the Kinetic Logistics Compound on Thides III

Overview

A device of unknown origin, patched into the navigation computer of the Stolen Alien-Tech Transport. Its faceted body carries shifting three-dimensional symbols that change in response to navigation inputs — the same family of technology the crew have now seen three times: the Kinetic Logistics Container in Processing II, the hexagonal jammer at Relay Station 11, and this.

Shackleton Magellan spent the three-hour flight back to MacMillian Station IV working it out. His conclusion: it permits hyperspace navigation of extraordinary precision without beacons, mapping coordinates off gravity wells rather than off the Republic’s lane markers — and it can plot a jump directly into a planetary atmosphere without the catastrophic risk that normally makes such a maneuver suicide. That is a capability beyond anything the Federal Republican Interseller Fleet has fielded, and it explains the vortex-drop the crew watched land inside the Kinetic Logistics Compound in Session 3 without tripping a single sentinel satellite.

Together with the vortex engines that provide the thrust, this is the other half of gateless FTL: the engines punch the hole, this decides where the hole comes out.

A tangle of wires ran from the device toward the rear of the ship, to modifications nobody got to examine. Whatever they were went with it.

Properties

  • Navigation: beaconless hyperspace plotting of extraordinary precision; coordinates mapped from gravity wells.
  • Atmospheric insertion: jumps directly into a planetary atmosphere, without the usual catastrophic risk.
  • Interface: shifting three-dimensional symbols across a faceted surface, responsive to nav inputs.
  • Provenance: unknown. The crew classify it as “technology of unknown origin” — they have not established it as non-human.

Appearances

  • Session 3 ([Session 03 - Buckle Up Motherfuckers](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 03/session-03-buckle-up-motherfuckers.html)) — Found by Six in the transport’s cockpit during the compound infiltration, with coordinates logging a recent atmospheric jump.
  • Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — Studied in detail by Shackleton Magellan on the flight home; its function established. Reported to Holger Graeme in flight and to Julija Borja under interrogation. Quarantined with the ship at Docking Bay 36 on the Marshal’s order — and then gone, when the ship left the Thides System.

Relationships

  • Part of Stolen Alien-Tech Transport — Wired into the transport's navigation computer; left the system with the hull.
  • Part of Vortex Engine — The navigation half of the gateless-jump capability the vortex engines provide.
