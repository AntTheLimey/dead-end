Stolen Alien-Tech Transport

vehicle

Current Holder: GONE — impounded at Docking Bay 36 under federal quarantine, then removed from the Thides System by persons unknown

Origin: Seized from the Kinetic Logistics Compound on Thides III

Overview

An older, heavily modified transport the crew stole from the Kinetic Logistics Compound on Thides III — and, for the first time since Processing II, hard, portable proof of everything they’ve seen. Its passenger interior was gutted for cargo, with unfamiliar modifications bolted on near the engines. In the cockpit, Six found alien technology wired directly into the navigation computer, with coordinates logging a recent jump straight into the planet’s atmosphere — bypassing the orbital satellite net entirely. The drives that did it are illegal vortex engines, the kind normally found only on large military vessels, that punch holes into hyperspace without a gate.

The hull itself is a Horizon Aerospace ST-160 — an upper-medium-tier starship built for atmospheric landings, the kind of ship companies buy when they want something a step above bare-bones without paying for a luxury yacht. This one has been stripped of that comfort and run hard: passenger fittings torn out for cargo, unfamiliar modifications bolted on near the drives.

The rear compartment held blocks of alien technology — “bricks” a technician was still wiring when Six ambushed him — rigged to external detonator lines that ran down the gangplank. Six spotted the wires on the way in; the crew reeled them inside before they could be triggered. Ronin Sanchez flew the hull out of the compound under fire, dodging biological blobs on the climb, and set course for MacMillian Station IV with the prisoner aboard.

Properties

  • Hull class: Horizon Aerospace ST-160 (upper-medium-tier, atmosphere-capable), older and beat-up; cargo-converted; bolt-on engine modifications.
  • Propulsion: illegal vortex engines — gateless hyperspace jumps.
  • Navigation: alien-integrated nav computer; last jump logged directly into atmosphere.
  • Cargo: alien “bricks,” formerly rigged to external detonators (now defused).
  • Aboard: Marlo Petrak, bound (delivered into federal custody on arrival).
  • Current status: gone. Quarantined at Docking Bay 36, then removed from the system; the Stealth Tracking Gizmo on its transponder relay returns no response.

Appearances

  • Session 3 ([Session 03 - Buckle Up Motherfuckers](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 03/session-03-buckle-up-motherfuckers.html)) — Scouted, boarded, and stolen from the Kinetic Logistics Compound; flown off-world as the crew’s new vessel and their first solid evidence.
  • Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — Lost. Flown home on the last of its thrust fuel, hailed en route by Julija Borja and ordered in under transponder. Shackleton Magellan used the three-hour flight to fit a covert Stealth Tracking Gizmo to its transponder relay and to work out what the Alien Navigation Device in its nav computer actually does. On arrival it was impounded and quarantined at Docking Bay 36 on the Marshal’s order and locked down by Holger Graeme. When Shackleton later pinged the tracker, nothing answered — the hull had already left the Thides System, taking the alien navigation device, the illegal vortex engines, and the hold of alien technology with it.
