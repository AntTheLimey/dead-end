Six's Mission Report

An in-world document produced in play. Six wrote it during the three-hour flight back from Thides III aboard the Stolen Alien-Tech Transport and submitted it twice: to Solveig Kaufer as the crew’s preliminary report on arrival, and to Julija Borja the following morning under federal interrogation.

What it is

An exhaustively detailed and rigorously efficient written account of everything the crew saw and did on Thides III — organised with numbered paragraphs and subsections, each observation and action individually referenced. Six wrote it the way he does everything: completely, without ornament, and on the assumption that having written a thing down once should end the matter.

What it contains

  • The Kinetic Logistics logos across the illegal Kinetic Logistics Compound.
  • The sighting of Daniela Akkermans commanding the compound from its command building.
  • The Alien Navigation Device found integrated into the transport’s navigation computer, with coordinates logging a jump directly into the planetary atmosphere.
  • The sabotaged Relay Station 11 — the alien jammer wired into the communications array, and the two years of falsified maintenance logs Jareth Brand uncovered.
  • The circumstances under which the crew commandeered the ship now impounded at Docking Bay 36.
  • Delivered verbally alongside the report: that Hildebrand Panza, recorded on Royale Hotel security footage among the human cleaning crew that scrubbed Room 2002, was one of the men who cleared Security Gate 1 with Daniela Akkermans in Session 1 — putting the Akker Cartel on both the Kinetic Logistics Compound and a station cover-up.

Why it matters

With the Stolen Alien-Tech Transport gone from the Thides System, this document, Six’s recordings from inside the compound, and Shackleton Magellan’s analysis are what the crew have left. It is the first federal record tying Kinetic Logistics to an illegal installation on a restricted world — and it exists in at least two places outside the crew’s control: MacMillian’s station files via Solveig Kaufer, and the Marshal’s Office via Julija Borja.

Appearances

  • Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — Written in transit, filed with Solveig Kaufer at the debrief, and worked line by line by Julija Borja during the interrogation. Six spent that interview citing his own paragraph and subsection numbers back at her, visibly exasperated at being asked to verbally confirm material he had already written down.
