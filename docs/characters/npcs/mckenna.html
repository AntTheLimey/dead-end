McKenna

Role Escort, The Silk Abbey Status alive
An escort at The Silk Abbey and one of the women summoned to the Royale Hotel party the night Brianna Melvin disappeared.

Overview

An escort at The Silk Abbey and one of the women summoned to the Royale Hotel party the night Brianna Melvin disappeared. Charming and easy with information — she talked freely under Rock Lavey’s private interviews and gave the crew the clearest picture of the evening: the women were called to entertain the station manager and his guest, and Brianna was specifically asked to stay behind when the rest were dismissed. She confirmed the arrangement was routine, once or twice a month, and mentioned the trip Agramunt had taken Brianna on to Aethel.

Beneath the easy gossip her account carried a note of unease: three days was longer than the pattern allowed for.
