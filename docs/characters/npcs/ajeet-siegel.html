Ajeet Siegel

Role Leader of Ajeet Scanning Status alive
Owner of the scanning operation named after him, he runs this one man show.

Overview

Owner of the scanning operation named after him, he runs this one man show. Ajeet’s been willing to take a percentage of the operation instead of pay. This gives him an extra income source, but ties him to the market. More so than other operations like his.
