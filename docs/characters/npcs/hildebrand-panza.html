Overview

A man recorded on Royale Hotel security footage as one of the human cleaning crew who entered Room 2002 after Brianna Melvin failed to leave it, and who spent thirty to forty-five minutes inside before departing. Shackleton Magellan recognised him on sight from a station security scan he had run months earlier — a scan whose military service record came back redacted.

He is one of Daniela Akkermans’s people. Six identified him in his report to Julija Borja as one of the men who accompanied Akkermans through Security Gate 1 during the crew’s first shift in Session 1 — the group who all cleared screening on honorable-discharge Federal Marine papers with service records almost entirely blacked out by the Federal Republic. That makes the cleaning crew that scrubbed the station manager’s suite the same faces the crew watched walk aboard with the Akker Cartel’s commander.

Pia Kadyrov did not recognise the name and agreed to dig into it. What remains open is who employed him for the hotel job, where he is now, and what the crew actually removed from the suite.
