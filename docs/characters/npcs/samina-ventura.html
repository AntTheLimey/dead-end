Samina Ventura

Overview

A retired prostitute that once worked in some of the highest class pleasure houses in the Federal Republic, Samina took her life savings and bought The Silk Abbey on MacMillian Station IV. She attempted to raise the quality, and the class, but there is only so much to do with the station in the state it is. She manages to attract the best girls on the station, with a higher level of cliental, and fairer operating fees.

She maintains a special relation with the Station Manager, and this allows her to quietly offer more risky services for more income. This includes looking the other ways as the dealers ply their drugs in the establishment, provided she gets a kickback. Station security is on to her, but as long as the illegal activities are confined to The Silk Abbey, Samina has nothing to worry about as security has other bigger problems.

Appearances

  • Session 1 (Session 01 - The Ragged Edge of Space) — Holger Graeme named Samina Ventura to the crew as proprietor of The Silk Abbey and a high-priority security client who pays for top-tier support. When the crew visited the Abbey that evening, the establishment’s madam greeted them warmly and offered each a complimentary service — the same quiet kickback the crew had watched operate all across the entertainment district, keeping security’s goodwill comfortable and unspoken.
  • Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — The session’s client. She approached Shackleton Magellan privately in The Silk Abbey and hired the crew to find Brianna Melvin, one of her courtesans, missing three days after a private party Wilton Agramunt hosted for a visiting dignitary — offering federal credits or free services, and supplying a photograph of Brianna that later let Shackleton identify Brianna’s Earring. On the crew’s report of the scrubbed suite, the missing bedding, and the earring, she concluded Brianna was dead or permanently gone; her response was measured and commercial. She told the crew she had not seen Wilton Agramunt since the previous day, last seen speaking with Ajeet Siegel, paid the crew 200 physical federal credits each, and offered to double the fee for proof that Peter Goodwin was involved — making clear she wanted it as financial leverage. Shackleton Magellan read her motive plainly: Brianna is a lost asset, Goodwin a deep pocket. See The Room 2002 Cover-Up.
