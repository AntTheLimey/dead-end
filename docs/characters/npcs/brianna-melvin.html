Brianna Melvin

Overview

A registered courtesan at The Silk Abbey and, by the open gossip of her colleagues, the particular favourite of the station manager — requested by name, kept back after the other women were dismissed, and taken off-station on at least one occasion to the city of Aethel in the Eris System, from which she returned with expensive gifts and fashionable clothes. Her colleagues described these disappearances as routine, happening once or twice a month.

She has a young child and a mother living in Processing I.

Three days after a private party in Room 2002 of the Royale Hotel, she had not returned. Samina Ventura judged the silence unlike her and hired the crew to find her. The hotel’s security footage records her entering the suite and never records her leaving; the room was subsequently scrubbed with ammonia by a human cleaning crew, its bedding removed, and its corridor and elevator cameras taken offline. The only trace of her recovered from the suite was a single earring (Brianna’s Earring) wedged behind the bed’s headboard.

Her fate is unestablished. Samina Ventura concluded on the crew’s report that she is dead or permanently gone; no body has been found, and the trash chutes’ biological sensors registered nothing.
