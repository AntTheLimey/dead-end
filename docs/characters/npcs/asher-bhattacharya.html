Overview

Leader of one of the two crews of the Syndicate in the Thides system, he is more of the muscle fo the boss. Concerned with the protection, debt collection, and other forms of intimidation if Asher shows up lookind for you it’ll probably be a bad time

Motivations & Secrets

Appearances

  • Session 1 (Session 01 - The Ragged Edge of Space) — Named only, not seen in person. Holger Graeme flagged Asher Bhattacharya to the crew during his briefing as dangerous muscle operating in the orbit of the Nova Nexus and the Serene Syndicate, and a person of interest for station security.
  • Session 2 ([Session 02 - The MacMillian Anomaly](…/…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 02/session-02-the-macmillian-anomaly.html)) — Met the crew in person for the first time, in a run-down back room at the Nova Nexus. He cooperated, showing security footage of Harsha Trent’s chip-skim scam, and gave two leads — Vanadís Baumhauer and Surendra Antonescu — on condition that his name never be attached to them.
