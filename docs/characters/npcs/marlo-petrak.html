Marlo Petrak

Role Technician, compound wiring for Kinetic Logistics Status alive
Marlo Petrak's day had just taken a horrible turn.

Overview

Marlo Petrak’s day had just taken a horrible turn. Charged with finishing the wiring on the last of the alien bricks in the transport’s sealed rear compartment, working late and alone because the job was behind and nobody liked being near the things, it was his misfortune to run into Six. Not that he saw him. That was the whole problem.

One moment there was the hum of the compartment and the cold weight of a brick in his hands. The next there was a hand over his mouth, an arm across his throat, and the deck coming up to meet his face while a man he never got a look at rode him down with the patient economy of someone who had done this before. He went under counting, and lost.

He woke trussed in his own gauge wire, which he might have found funny on a better night. Outside, the compound was ending. Something had walked through it like a hand sweeping a table clear, and through the hull he could hear the exact music of everything he understood going wrong at once, shouted orders, dead systems whining, small-arms fire pattering off the plating like rain. Then strangers were coming up the ramp he had helped power, someone dropped into the pilot’s seat, and a voice told everyone to buckle up.

They took him with them. Petrak spent the climb to orbit lashed in a hold full of the same alien bricks he had just spent a shift wiring, while a pilot he had never met threw the ship sideways to dodge two blobs of the substance that eats metal on contact. He does not know these people. He does not know where they are taking him. He knows he is alive, which on the evidence of the last hour he has decided to count as a win, and that wherever this is going, he is going too. As cargo. To be asked things.

Motivations & Secrets

Open. Un-statted and un-interrogated as of session end. He was wiring the alien bricks and the detonator lines when Six took him, so he was close enough to the work to be worth questioning. What he knows about Kinetic Logistics, the alien tech, and the vortex hardware is entirely to be determined. A working technician pulled off a job, not a true believer, so what he gives up is a question of how he is handled.
