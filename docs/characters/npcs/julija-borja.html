Julija Borja

Overview

Ambitious Marshal of the Federal Republic, she is a by the book official and finds it difficult operating in a company controlled system. Her lack of direct authority outside of limited areas forces her to spend most time doing paperwork reporting actions of the company, as well as arranging the deportations from the Thides System. She is effectively the only representative of the Federal Republic in the system.

Appearances

  • Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — First contact with the crew, and a hard one. She hailed the Stolen Alien-Tech Transport on its way back from Thides III demanding identification, accepted the crew’s claim to be MacMillian security on authorised orders, and ordered them to run their transponder and bring the ship in — then had it quarantined at Docking Bay 36 on arrival while she flew a Marshal-marked military fighter down to Thides III herself to investigate the interrupted relays. The following morning she interrogated the crew in her office at the Marshal’s Office, with Marlo Petrak in the brig behind her. When Six refused to answer on grounds of classified corporate orders and cited Holger Graeme’s instructions and station regulation clauses at her, she walked across the hall and berated Graeme in a voice that carried through the walls until he came back and ordered the crew to cooperate fully. She then worked Six’s Mission Report with pedantic thoroughness — verbally re-verifying material already written down, to Six’s visible exasperation — covering the Kinetic Logistics logos at the Kinetic Logistics Compound, the sighting of Daniela Akkermans, and the Alien Navigation Device. She broke off abruptly on connecting the technology at Relay Station 11, the container in Processing II, and the ship, dismissed the crew, and resumed pressing Holger Graeme on why her office had been kept in the dark.
