Overview

A “business” man that runs an investment firm located out of Aethel.

Motivations & Secrets

Appearances

  • Session 2 ([Session 02 - The MacMillian Anomaly](…/…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 02/session-02-the-macmillian-anomaly.html)) — Visiting from Goodwin Investments (Aethel, Eris System), he was entertained at The Silk Abbey and observed gambling under heavy security at the Nova Nexus. The station manager broke off the crew’s post-incident debrief to attend to him, prioritizing his comfort over the Processing II security breach, and the crew’s sudden reassignment to Thides III the next morning looked to them like a maneuver to get them off-station during his visit.
  • Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — Named but not met, and by session’s end the crew’s prime suspect by commission. He was the dignitary Wilton Agramunt threw the Room 2002 party for, the night Brianna Melvin disappeared. Security footage shows him leaving the suite with Agramunt and Solveig Kaufer, the two men heading toward The Silk Abbey. Three Goodwin Investments business cards on the dresser identify the untouched right bedroom as his — which is what gives meaning to the scrubbed left one — and Róbert’s Engraved Watch was found beside them, a gift from The Bamboo Union’s treasurer Róbert Jeanes. Samina Ventura offered the crew a doubled fee for proof of his involvement in Brianna’s disappearance, intending to use it for blackmail or legal leverage. See The Room 2002 Cover-Up.
