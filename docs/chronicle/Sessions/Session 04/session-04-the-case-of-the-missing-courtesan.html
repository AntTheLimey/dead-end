Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan

4 Sat Jul 25 2026 20:00:00 GMT-0400 (Eastern Daylight Time) reviewed

Fourth session of Chapter 1 - The Far Dark, run cold (no formal prep file, as with Sessions 1–3). The stolen transport from [Session 3](…/Session 03/session-03-buckle-up-motherfuckers.html) reaches MacMillian Station IV — and the evidence is immediately taken out of the crew’s hands, first by Julija Borja’s quarantine order and then by the ship’s disappearance from the system entirely. The session’s body is a station-side missing-persons investigation for Samina Ventura at The Silk Abbey, which walks the crew into a professional cover-up reaching the station manager’s office — and closes with Kinetic Logistics answering the compound raid with an armed ambush on station.
