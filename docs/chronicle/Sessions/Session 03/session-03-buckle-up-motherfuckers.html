Session 03 - Buckle Up Motherfuckers

3 Sat Jul 18 2026 20:00:00 GMT-0400 (Eastern Daylight Time) reviewed

Third session of Chapter 1 - The Far Dark, run cold (no formal prep file, as with Sessions 1 and 2). The Thides III maintenance-escort detail from the end of [Session 2](…/Session 02/session-02-the-macmillian-anomaly.html) turns into the chapter’s first hard payoff: a routine relay-servicing run down a lush, EM-lethal world goes wrong, and the crew fights through jungle predators to uncover a hidden Kinetic Logistics compound running the same alien technology they saw in Processing II — guarded by Daniela Akkermans’s Akker Cartel. They leave with a stolen ship full of evidence and a live prisoner. This hub holds metadata and links only — see the linked documents.
