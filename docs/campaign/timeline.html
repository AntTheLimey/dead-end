Timeline

In-world chronology of established events. In-game dates fill in as Tim pins them; until then, entries are ordered by session.

Chapter 1 — The Far Dark

  • (in-game date TBD) — Session 1 — Session 01 - The Ragged Edge of Space — The crew (Six, Rock Lavey, Shackleton Magellan, Ronin Sanchez) arrive at MacMillian Station IV by separate routes and take the MacMillian Security job. Orientation under Solveig Kaufer, the real briefing from Chief Holger Graeme, a first gate shift, and a night through the Entertainment District (The Comets Tail, The Silk Abbey, Nova Nexus). Over the following weeks the crew maps the station’s selective-enforcement corruption via the Law Machine and clocks the local players: the Rusted Blades under Leocadia Zavia, the Serene Syndicate around the Nova Nexus, and the redacted-record Marine Daniela Akkermans.

    • (in-game date TBD) — The Coordinate Shakedown — at The Comets Tail, the crew watch Leocadia Zavia extract coordinates from a captive miner; Shackleton Magellan’s bot-tap is blown by Ronin Sanchez before the numbers are heard. An open lead.

  • (in-game date TBD) — Session 2 — [Session 02 - The MacMillian Anomaly](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 02/session-02-the-macmillian-anomaly.html) — After weeks as the station’s standing TAC team, the crew work a scam case (Harsha Trent) through Station 45 and the Nova Nexus, then a 4 A.M. alarm sends them into mothballed Processing II. They find a cargo container (Kinetic Logistics Container) rigged with a Mysterious Relay bridging human and non-human technology. Six boards it as it’s dragged into space by a hijacked drone, guts the relay, carves out two Alien Metal Chunks before Corrosive Goo and a timed self-destruct take the container into a “vortex.” The samples are confiscated to Zigurds Sloan; the crew is confined, then reassigned to Thides III.

    • (in-game date TBD) — The Processing II Anomaly — the container incident itself: covert repeated access to a sealed sector, disabled station drones, apparent first-contact technology, and a station cover-up that prioritizes investor Peter Goodwin over the breach.

  • (in-game date TBD) — Session 3 — [Session 03 - Buckle Up Motherfuckers](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 03/session-03-buckle-up-motherfuckers.html) — The crew and technician Jareth Brand fly a stripped-down Type 113A transport to restricted Thides III on a relay-maintenance escort. Mid-run their ship is downed by a dissolving substance; Ronin Sanchez crash-lands in the jungle. Fighting off EM-drawn wildlife (Furry Lampreys, a Chitinoteuthis), the crew reach Relay Station 11 and find it falsely logged as serviced for two years, with an alien device jamming comms — and spot a hidden Kinetic Logistics Compound taking a vortex-jump delivery. They raid the compound (guarded by Daniela Akkermans’s Akker Cartel), steal a transport rigged with alien tech and vortex engines, take a prisoner, and escape to MacMillian Station IV.

    • (in-game date TBD) — The Kinetic Logistics Compound Raid — the infiltration, EMP strike, ship theft, and armed getaway; the crew’s first hard physical evidence since Processing II.

  • (in-game date TBD) — Session 4 — [Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html) — The crew fly the stolen transport home on its last fuel, are intercepted and cleared by Marshal Julija Borja, and dock at MacMillian Station IV — where Holger Graeme impounds the ship under federal quarantine at Docking Bay 36. Shackleton Magellan works out that the Alien Navigation Device permits beaconless jumps straight into atmosphere, and fits a Stealth Tracking Gizmo to the hull. Granted 24 hours of R&R, the crew are hired by Samina Ventura to find the missing courtesan Brianna Melvin, and trade information with reporter Pia Kadyrov of Thides Cast. Julija Borja interrogates them, receives Six’s Mission Report, and confronts Holger Graeme over MacMillian’s reporting chain. On patrol that evening, a Kinetic Logistics team ambushes them on the cargo level. Shackleton’s tracker returns nothing: the quarantined ship has left the Thides System.

    • (in-game date TBD) — The Room 2002 Cover-Up — Brianna Melvin vanishes after a Royale Hotel party hosted by Wilton Agramunt for Peter Goodwin; the suite is professionally scrubbed, the cameras timed around it, and Wilton Agramunt himself goes missing days later.
    • (in-game date TBD) — The Shipping Sector Ambush — armed Kinetic Logistics operatives jam comms and attack the crew in the Shipping and Receiving Sector; one killed, one captured alive, one fled, and a Kinetic Logistics ID Card recovered.
