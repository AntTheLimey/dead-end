De Jonker Security Solutions

Goals

    Overview

    Their legitimate business as mercenaries for higher to protect your shipments, in reality they take on illegal contracts by companies to disrupt those very shipments. Their criminal activity is an open secret, but it is often ignored by corporations as they provide a useful service as a deniable proxy for corporations to attack their rivals. They will take most any contract, provided it doesn’t cross too close to Federal Republican entities, the only real threat to their operations
