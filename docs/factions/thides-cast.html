Thides Cast

corporation

Goals

    Overview

    A small operation run by Pia Kadyrov in the Thides system. Mostly run out of her domicile, the operation is scraping by on mostly re-broadcast content from the major networks.

    History

    Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — First contact with the crew. Pia Kadyrov chased them through the Entertainment District for a statement on the Processing II container incident and was refused; the crew later came to her at the Thides Cast Studio and traded instead. She now holds the Royale Hotel footage, Hildebrand Panza’s name to research, and the knowledge that the crew have encountered technology of unknown origin — which she recognised immediately as the career-making story she came to the Thides System hoping for.
