Station 45 Gang

criminal

Territory: Station 45

Goals

  • Hold the Station 45 turf along [[The Main Line]]
  • Run the illegal fighting ring in the hidden maintenance area
  • Collect on debts owed to the bookies working the same turf

Members

  • Alphonse
  • Gio

Overview

The local street gang that controls Station 45, a stretch of territory along The Main Line in Processing I. The turf takes its name from the numbered markings still visible on the original processing bulkheads, and the gang takes its name from the turf.

Tucked into an old maintenance area inside that territory is the entrance to an illegal fighting ring, run by the gang and used to collect debts from the bookies working the same turf. The station security camera covering the entrance has been deliberately tilted away from the doorway, leaving a narrow blind spot that the footage does not quite cover.

Known members are Gio and Alphonse. Neither is in charge of anything, and no leadership has been identified.

Goals & Methods

They hold ground and they collect. When Harsha Trent owed their bookies and tried to scam his way out of it, Gio and Alphonse threw him out into a pile of trash, and he went on to lose more, borrow more, and end up on a deadline to pay.

Their response to attention is numbers rather than violence. When Six and Rock Lavey came down asking about Trent, the members present first tried to fade into the background, then talked without giving anything up, and then roughly ten more drifted into a loose perimeter around the pair until they left.

Resources
