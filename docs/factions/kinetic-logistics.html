Kinetic Logistics

corporation

Territory: Kinetic Logistics Compound on restricted Thides III

Goals

    Overview

    A corporation whose name the crew first met as a label — the shipping logo and countdown-terminal tag on the Kinetic Logistics Container found in Processing II ([Session 2](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 02/session-02-the-macmillian-anomaly.html)). In [Session 3](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 03/session-03-buckle-up-motherfuckers.html) the name became a place: an illegal compound on restricted Thides III, hidden in the world’s EM-hazard shadow, moving grown alien technology and illegal vortex engines off the surface on ships that jump in and out of the atmosphere without tripping the satellite net. The same dissolving substance that scrubbed the Processing II container’s evidence is used here as a weapon — it downed the crew’s transport. Its security on the ground is provided by the Akker Cartel, with Daniela Akkermans observed commanding from the compound.

    Goals & Methods

    Player-side unknown. Observably: covert extraction and transfer of alien tech from a restricted world, under cover of a falsified relay-maintenance record (Relay Station 11 logged as serviced for two years by an unknown name), with cartel muscle and self-destruct/evidence-scrub discipline.

    Resources

    At least two ships at the compound (a newer Kinetic Logistics cargo vessel and an older alien-tech transport, the latter now stolen by the crew), a warehouse of cargo, ~50–60 workers, vortex-capable hulls, and Akker Cartel security.

    History

    • Session 2 — name appears on the Kinetic Logistics Container in Processing II.
    • Session 3 — the Kinetic Logistics Compound on Thides III is discovered and raided; the crew steal an alien-tech transport and take a technician prisoner.

    Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — Escalated from a name on evidence to an active armed presence on MacMillian Station IV. Days after the crew raided the Kinetic Logistics Compound on Thides III, an armoured team ambushed them on routine patrol in the Shipping and Receiving Sector: a tripwire and trigger box, total communications jamming, gas canisters, and beam weapons that burn through reinforced station plating. The crew killed one, captured one alive, and recovered a Kinetic Logistics ID Card from the dead man — the first documentary proof tying the company to violence aboard the station. See The Shipping Sector Ambush. In the same period the Stolen Alien-Tech Transport the crew took from the compound left the Thides System out of a federal quarantine, taking the Alien Navigation Device and the vortex engines with it.

    Relationships

    • Owns Kinetic Logistics Compound — Runs the illegal alien-tech compound on Thides III.
    • Owns Beam Pistol — Carried by the Kinetic Logistics ambush team.
    • Owns Electronic Trigger Box — Part of the Kinetic Logistics ambush setup.
    • Allied with Akker Cartel — Akker Cartel provides security/muscle at the compound (Daniela Akkermans present).
