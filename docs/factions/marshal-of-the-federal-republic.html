Marshal of the Federal Republic

government

Goals

    Members

    • Julija Borja

    Overview

    The most common federal official people have interactions with, they represent the law and order of the Federal Republic. There will be at least one assigned to every occupied system in the Federal Republic. Marshals are authorized to deputize locals to serve under them as needed. In addition, Marshals are authorized to enforce federal law even against the local authorities. When a citizen is arrested by local authorities, one of the first contacts is the local Marshals.

    History

    Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — Julija Borja, the sole Marshal in the Thides System, intercepted the crew’s inbound flight, quarantined the Stolen Alien-Tech Transport at Docking Bay 36, flew a Marshal-marked military fighter to Thides III to inspect the interrupted relays, and formally interrogated the crew the following morning — receiving Six’s Mission Report and, with it, the first federal record of Kinetic Logistics operating on a restricted world. She also established, loudly, that MacMillian’s reporting chain has been keeping her office in the dark. The quarantined ship subsequently left the system.
