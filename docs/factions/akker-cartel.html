Akker Cartel

other

Leadership: Daniela Akkermans

Goals

    Overview

    A small group operating in Sector-7G that have recently focused on the Thides System. They mostly operate as organized muscle for hire, but have taken other jobs. Their expertise seems to be around providing security and extraction operations. They have strong ties to the Federal Military.

    Goals & Methods

    Resources

    History

    • Session 1 — Daniela Akkermans and three companions, all ex-Federal Marines with redacted records, pass through station security (Session 01 - The Ragged Edge of Space).
    • Session 3 — the cartel is found providing security at the Kinetic Logistics Compound on restricted Thides III; Daniela Akkermans observed commanding it. The crew raided the compound and escaped ([Session 03 - Buckle Up Motherfuckers](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 03/session-03-buckle-up-motherfuckers.html)).

    Session 4 ([Session 04 - The Case of the Missing Courtesan](…/chronicle/Sessions/Session 04/session-04-the-case-of-the-missing-courtesan.html)) — Placed inside the station’s own crimes for the first time. Six identified Hildebrand Panza, one of the human cleaning crew recorded scrubbing Room 2002 after Brianna Melvin vanished, as one of the men who cleared Security Gate 1 alongside Daniela Akkermans in Session 1 — and reported it to Julija Borja. Until this point the cartel was muscle for the Kinetic Logistics Compound on Thides III, five miles of jungle away from anything the crew could touch. It is now, on the crew’s own testimony, mixed up in a cover-up on the station involving the station manager’s private party. See The Room 2002 Cover-Up.

    Relationships
